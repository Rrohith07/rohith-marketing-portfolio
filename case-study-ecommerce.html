E-COMMERCE · CASE STUDY

Applying Performance Marketing to My Own Business

Running paid advertising for my own e-commerce business with direct responsibility for budget, targeting, creative and campaign decisions.

THE OVERVIEW

From marketer to business owner.

Unlike client campaigns, this project puts the complete advertising decision-making process in my hands.

I independently plan campaigns, decide how budget is allocated, work with targeting and creatives, launch campaigns and review their performance.

ROLE Founder / Marketer
BUSINESS E-Commerce
OWNERSHIP End-to-End
FOCUS Paid Acquisition

WHAT I OWN

Every part of the advertising decision.

01

Budget

Decide how advertising budget is allocated and adjust spending based on campaign performance.

02

Campaign Setup

Plan and launch advertising campaigns rather than working only on existing campaign structures.

03

Targeting

Select and refine audiences based on the customers the business needs to reach.

04

Creative

Make decisions around the advertising creatives used to present products to potential customers.

05

Performance

Review metrics such as CTR, CPC and conversions to understand campaign behaviour and guide decisions.

06

Optimization

Use campaign performance to refine targeting, spending and creative decisions.

WHY THIS PROJECT MATTERS

Working with my own money changes the perspective.

When the advertising budget belongs to the same business I own, every campaign decision has a direct consequence.

It pushes me to look beyond individual metrics and think about how budget, targeting, creative and customer behaviour work together.

E-commerce business and campaign work.

MY RESPONSIBILITY

End-to-end ownership.

Campaign Planning
Budget Management
Audience Targeting
Creative Decisions
Campaign Optimization
Performance Review

WHAT I TOOK FROM THIS EXPERIENCE

Running advertising feels different when every marketing decision affects a business you personally own.

It gives me a different perspective from managing client campaigns because there is no separation between the marketing decision and its financial consequence.
