THE OVERVIEW

Starting from a blank campaign.

The campaign was created from the ground up for a local service business that depended on customers searching for its services within a specific area.

My focus was to create the initial campaign, identify relevant keywords and continuously refine targeting using actual campaign data.

ROLE Campaign Setup & Optimization
PLATFORM Google Ads — Search
FOCUS Local Lead Generation

THE CHALLENGE

Finding relevant local traffic.

Getting clicks was not the only objective. The campaign needed to reach people whose searches showed genuine intent to use the service.

That required continuous refinement of keywords, search terms and location targeting.
