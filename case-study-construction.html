Multi-Channel Lead Generation for a Construction Business

Managing Google Search and Meta advertising together to generate customer actions across both search and social platforms.

~120 Google Conversions
30 Meta Conversations
2 Paid Channels

THE OVERVIEW

One business goal. Two acquisition channels.

The business wanted to generate enquiries online rather than depend on a single advertising channel.

I worked across both Google Ads and Meta Ads, managing campaigns while communicating directly with the business owner about campaign objectives and results.

ROLE Paid Ads Management
PLATFORMS Google + Meta
INDUSTRY Construction
FOCUS Lead Generation

THE APPROACH

Different platforms. Different customer behaviour.

01

Google Search

Search campaigns were used to reach people already searching for relevant construction services.

02

Meta Advertising

Facebook and Instagram campaigns were used to reach targeted audiences and generate direct customer conversations.

03

Performance Monitoring

Monitored both channels and compared campaign behaviour and cost-per-result across platforms.

04

Client Communication

Worked directly with the business owner to connect advertising activity with real sales objectives.

WHAT I TOOK FROM THIS CAMPAIGN

The same business can require very different advertising strategies depending on where the customer is coming from.

Google Search allowed the campaign to reach people with existing search intent, while Meta provided another path for generating customer conversations. Managing both also gave me practical experience comparing different acquisition channels for the same business.
